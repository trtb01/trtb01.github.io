Capability summaries

a6000 — 2014

24MP · 179-pt PDAF · 11fps · no IBIS

24MP APS-C, phase-detect AF, tilting screen, built-in EVF, hot shoe. No IBIS, no mic jack, no subject recognition. AF is fine for static subjects and loses moving subjects quickly; struggles badly in low light. Still a genuinely good camera and outstanding value.

a6300 — 2016

24MP · 425-pt PDAF · 4K · no IBIS

Large AF upgrade: 425 phase-detect points across most of the frame, faster and stickier than the a6000. Adds 4K video, mic jack, magnesium alloy body, better sealing. Cheapest meaningful AF upgrade in the line.

a6500 — 2016

24MP · 425-pt PDAF · IBIS · touchscreen

a6300 plus 5-axis IBIS, touchscreen, larger buffer, better heat handling. IBIS makes handheld low-light and long-lens work far more viable. Still the pre-AI AF generation.

a6400 — 2019

24MP · Real-time Tracking · no IBIS

The AF leap: Real-time Eye AF and Real-time Tracking hold a subject once acquired. Flip-up selfie screen, better processor. Best autofocus per dollar on the used market.

a6600 — 2019

24MP · Real-time Tracking · IBIS · Z-battery

a6400's AF plus IBIS, the big Z battery (roughly double the life), headphone jack, deeper grip. The best all-around APS-C Sony before the a6700.

a6700 — 2023

26MP · AI subject recognition · IBIS · 4K120

Current flagship. AI subject detection separately recognizes birds, animals, vehicles, insects, and people. 759-point AF, IBIS, front command dial, Z battery. The only Sony APS-C that reliably recognizes and holds focus on a bird.

a7S II — 2015 (full frame)

12.2MP · contrast-detect AF only

Extraordinary low light and video. But its autofocus is contrast-detect for stills tracking, so it is superb on static scenes and poor on birds, sport, and moving cars. The megapixel count is not the issue — 12MP prints fine past 8×10. The autofocus is.

Key takeaways

  1. Megapixels are not the constraint. 12MP is plenty for 4×6 and social media.
  2. AF generation is the real dividing line. a6000 → a6300/a6400 is a far bigger practical upgrade than any megapixel change.
  3. IBIS matters for dark venues and handheld telephoto. a6500, a6600, a6700 have it; a6000, a6300, a6400 do not.
  4. Subject recognition is what fixes birds — and only the a6700 has it.
  5. Glass is the bigger bottleneck for birds and sport than the body is.
